Dealing With Roof Leak Water Damage in Spartanburg

The spot on your ceiling is seldom directly below the hole in the roof. A loose shingle, a failed flashing joint or an aging seal around a skylight lets rain in, and it travels across the roof deck and framing until it falls into the insulation. Often the attic has been damp for some time before any brown mark reaches the room below. Locating all of the moisture and drying it is our job.

To be clear, roof repair is not what we do, water damage is. We work on the interior, which means removing wet insulation, drying drywall and framing, dealing with mold and rebuilding the ceiling. If rain is still coming in, we can tarp the roof as a temporary cover, or work alongside the roofer you choose so the leak is fixed before we close anything up.

Do This First

What to Do Right Now

  1. Shut Off Wet CircuitsIf water is dripping through a light fixture, a ceiling fan or near an outlet, switch off that circuit at the breaker and leave the fixture alone.
  2. Catch the DripsFurniture, electronics and rugs should be moved aside, with buckets placed wherever water is falling. Plastic or towels spread on the floor will keep it from soaking.
  3. Drain a Bulging CeilingWhen water pools in a bubble overhead, pierce the low point with a screwdriver while standing clear, and let it run into a bucket.
  4. Leave the Roof AloneClimbing on slick shingles or crawling around a dark attic is not worth the risk. Note from inside where the water is coming in and leave the rest to professionals.
  5. Document What You SeeBefore anything is moved or discarded, get video and photos of the attic, the ceiling and damaged items, and then phone us so drying can begin.

Common Causes

How It Usually Starts

  • A valley liner that has worn through at the point where the most runoff passes.
  • Water backing up at the eave and under the shingles because the gutters are blocked.
  • Skylight seals and curbs that dry out, crack and let rain run inside.
  • Nails that have backed out of the decking and left small holes through the shingles.
  • Sun-cracked rubber collars where plumbing vent pipes come through the roof.
If It Is Left

What Happens if You Wait

Hidden Attic Mold

A dark, warm attic with damp wood and paper-faced drywall suits mold well. Within 24 to 48 hours it can start growing, then move across the bottom of the roof deck.

Lights and Wiring

Recessed cans, ceiling fans and electrical boxes are low points where roof water collects and drips through. Moisture in a fixture can cause a short and later corrosion, which is why it needs inspection before use.

Attic Insulation

For days after a leak, matted insulation sits wet on top of the ceiling and no longer insulates. Fiberglass can sometimes be dried. Removal is the usual answer for wet cellulose.

Rafters and Decking

Wood in the roof structure that is soaked over and over darkens, swells and softens. Rot in these members weakens the structure that holds the roof up.

Coverage

Does Insurance Cover It?

Interior damage from a sudden, accidental roof leak, like one caused by a fallen limb or wind-torn shingles, is often covered under a homeowners policy. Leaks blamed on age, wear or skipped maintenance are usually denied, and the roof repair itself may be handled differently from the interior. Our team gives your adjuster photos and moisture readings, yet what is covered comes down to your policy, so check it or talk to your agent.

Pricing

What Affects the Cost

  • Attic access, ceiling height and whether a temporary tarp is needed first.
  • The amount of soaked insulation in the attic, and if it must come out.
  • How long the roof leaked before it was found, and any mold growth.
  • The amount of ceiling and wall drywall that needs to be cut out.
