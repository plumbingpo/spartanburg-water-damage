A sump pump that quits, a sewer that backs up, a pipe that lets go, rising river water, storm surge or a hard rain can each flood a property, and the damage should be handled by professionals without delay. Expect gray water (Category 2) or black water (Category 3) in almost any flood, and with it bacteria, contamination and potentially hazardous substances that threaten your health. Day or night, 24/7, Spartanburg Restoration sends flood cleanup crews throughout Spartanburg to remove floodwater, sanitize the affected rooms and safely restore your home or building.

Cleanup opens with rapid extraction: truck-mounted units and submersible pumps that pull thousands of gallons an hour out of the building. After that, IICRC-certified technicians on our team measure how contaminated the area is, then sanitize it with antimicrobial products, HEPA-filtered air cleaning and targeted disinfection of all surfaces the water reached. Materials beyond saving, like contaminated drywall, damaged insulation and saturated carpet pad, are removed and disposed of the right way.

Then the drying begins: high-velocity air movers and commercial dehumidifiers of the LGR (low-grain refrigerant) type go in to pull moisture out of the building's structural parts. Daily readings from calibrated moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera tell us when everything is fully dry, which is what keeps mold from growing. From start to finish we document each step for your insurance claim, with the moisture maps, equipment logs, photos and scope-of-work reports adjusters ask for.

Flood Cleanup Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Extend your downspouts to carry rain from the roof out past the foundation
  • Elevate appliances and electrical systems in flood-prone parts of your property
  • Check that grading on all sides of the house carries water away from the foundation
  • Keep gutters and storm drains clear of debris so water can flow properly
  • Install a battery backup sump pump so you stay protected when storms knock out power
  • Have a backwater valve fitted to the sewer line so sewage cannot flow back inside

Signs It Is Time for Flood Cleanup

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Receding water that leaves muddy residue and debris behind
  • Storm damage that lets rain in through the roof or walls
  • Water pooled on any floor of the building, no matter where it came from
  • Stains along baseboards and lower walls that mark the flood level
  • Basement flooding after the sump pump quit in a downpour
  • A sewer or drain backup pushing contaminated water into living spaces
  • Water coming in through foundation cracks, window wells or door thresholds

The Benefits of Professional Flood Cleanup

Getting the standing water out is only one part of cleaning up after a flood. The water brings sewage, chemicals, bacteria and more with it, and all of that is a serious hazard to health. Professional flood crews are trained in containment, personal protective equipment (PPE) and sanitizing protocols that keep your family from exposure to pathogens such as E. coli, hepatitis and other waterborne diseases.

Beyond the health concerns, a poor flood cleanup often leaves hidden moisture that turns into severe mold growth within days. With thermal imaging, psychrometric calculations and moisture meters, trained technicians confirm that subfloors, structural framing, wall cavities and every other wet area dry to an acceptable standard. Drying by measurement is the one dependable method for avoiding secondary damage, which can add thousands to the repair bill.
