Warning Signs for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Walls on the lower floor are wet from top to bottom because of a leak overhead.
  • Floor-level power strips, plugged-in appliances or the breaker panel are now wet.
  • Carpet, drywall or hardwood has been soaked for more than a few hours.
  • You see water escaping around a light fitting, from a wall outlet or through the ceiling.
  • You have no way to locate or get to the shutoff while water continues to pour.
  • What is on the floor came up from the sewer, or it is discolored and smells foul.
  • Gallons keep spilling from a ruptured water heater tank.

Emergency Water Damage Restoration Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Locate the main shutoff valve now, and show each person at home how to turn it off.
  • Going away longer than a few days? Close the main water valve first.
  • In basements and other low areas, keep documents and valuables up on shelves.
  • Wrap plumbing in cold, unheated parts of the house so a hard freeze cannot crack it.
  • Run a check on the sump pump ahead of wet weather, and think about adding battery backup.
  • Sinks, dishwashers, washing machines and water heaters are all good spots for a leak sensor.
